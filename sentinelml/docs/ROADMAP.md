# Research and engineering roadmap

1. MLflow experiment tracking and registry integration.
2. DVC data versioning and dataset lineage.
3. Prometheus metrics and Grafana dashboards.
4. Model rollback, signed artifacts, and atomic promotion.
5. Authenticated API, rate limiting, and structured logs.
6. Temporal/segment evaluation and calibration checks.
7. Drift simulations with measured false-positive rates.
8. Evaluate predictions on newly labelled outcomes.
9. Cloud deployment and infrastructure as code.
10. Expand the optional AI incident analyst with structured outputs, evidence references, evaluation cases, and latency/cost tracking. It must remain advisory-only.
